DivMaxOMG

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 (updated for accounts)

DivMaxOMG is a web app that turns your long videos into vertical short videos and, when you ask it to, posts them to your own TikTok account or sends them to your TikTok drafts. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

Where the app runs

The app is served from https://divmaxomg.divmaxomg.workers.dev, hosted by Cloudflare. Video cutting and editing happen entirely in your web browser: your videos are never uploaded to our server, except the shorts you explicitly choose to send to TikTok, which pass through our server on their way to TikTok and are not kept.

Data we store

  • Account. Your email address and a salted, one-way hash of your password (PBKDF2). We never store or see your password in clear text.
  • Sessions. When you sign in, a random session identifier is stored in a secure, HttpOnly cookie. Our database only keeps a hash of it and its expiry date (7 days).
  • TikTok connection. When you connect TikTok, we store your TikTok open ID and the access and refresh tokens TikTok gives us, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. These tokens are never sent to your browser.
  • Security logs. To block password-guessing, we keep failed sign-in and sign-up attempts (email address or IP address, and time) for at most 24 hours.
  • In your browser only. The shorts you create, your settings and your on-screen handle stay in your browser's local storage on your device.

How TikTok data is used

The app requests three TikTok permissions:

  • user.info.basic, to sign in and to show your TikTok avatar and nickname before you post.
  • video.publish, used only when you click "Publier" to post the shorts you selected, with the privacy level, interaction settings and commercial content disclosure you chose.
  • video.upload, used only when you choose to send shorts to your TikTok drafts.

TikTok data is never used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and is never sold or shared with anyone.

Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies: a session cookie to keep you signed in, and a short-lived cookie (10 minutes) to protect the TikTok connection step. Both are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and its database, under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok receives the shorts you choose to post or upload, under TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs deliver the app's fonts and its ZIP library. They can see your IP address when your browser downloads these files.

Retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account at any time in the app ("Supprimer mon compte"). This immediately deletes your email, password hash, sessions and TikTok tokens, and revokes the app's TikTok access.
  • "Se déconnecter" next to your TikTok account deletes your TikTok tokens and revokes access.
  • Shorts stay in your browser until you click "Tout effacer" or clear the site data.
  • You can also revoke access in TikTok: Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions.

Children

DivMaxOMG is not intended for children under 13, and must only be used by people allowed to hold a TikTok account under TikTok's own terms.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data at any time. Your account page shows the email address we hold, and "Supprimer mon compte" erases everything. For any other request, contact us as shown below.

Changes
